How we work · Field to decision

From a household interview to a ministry briefing.

Every engagement follows the same path. Scroll through the four stages.

STAGE 1 · IN THE FIELD

Collect

Trained enumerators run interviews on tablets with CSPro and KoboToolbox. Every case is GPS-stamped, checked on the device and synced to the server each day.

CAPI · GPS on every case · daily sync
STAGE 2 · IN THE PROCESSING ROOM

Clean

Structure and consistency checks run on every batch. Duplicate case IDs are resolved, household and member files are merged, and nothing moves forward until the totals reconcile.

Edit checks · de-duplication · validated merges
STAGE 3 · ON THE MAP

Map

Each record is joined to its county, district and enumeration area. Field coordinates are reconciled against boundaries so every number has a place.

ArcGIS Pro · EA boundaries · spatial joins
STAGE 4 · AT THE DECISION TABLE

Decide

Results arrive as dashboards, tables and short briefs that ministries, agencies and partners can act on the same week.

Dashboards · reports · policy briefs
