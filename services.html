Data

Survey & Data Collection

We design and run household, facility and market surveys from the first questionnaire draft to the final clean dataset. Interviews are captured on tablets, every case carries GPS coordinates, and data reaches the server daily so problems are caught while teams are still in the field.

  • Questionnaire design in CSPro and KoboToolbox
  • Sampling and listing
  • Enumerator recruitment and training
  • Field supervision and back-checks
  • Daily data sync and monitoring
  • Clean, documented datasets
Spatial

GIS & Spatial Data

Our GIS team builds the spatial frame that surveys and plans depend on. We prepare enumeration area maps for fieldwork, check boundaries against field data and produce maps and spatial analysis for planning and risk work.

  • Enumeration area maps for fieldwork
  • County, district and EA boundary files
  • Field GPS reconciliation
  • Thematic and hazard mapping
  • Spatial analysis in ArcGIS Pro
  • Map products for reports
Data

Resettlement Data Management

Resettlement action plans depend on accurate records of every affected household and asset. We manage that data from census to entitlement, with tools that hold up under tight timelines and difficult field conditions.

  • Census of project-affected persons
  • Asset and land inventories
  • Entitlement and compensation databases
  • Grievance and payment tracking
  • Data quality review
  • Reporting for RAP documents
