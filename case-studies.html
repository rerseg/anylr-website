Featured Case Study

Resettlement Action Plan Data Management — Cross-Border Infrastructure Project

A leading international environmental and social consultancy engaged All Your Needs to manage the complete data lifecycle for a major toll road RAP spanning two countries in Central and Southern Africa — covering 762 Project-Affected Persons under tight deadlines and challenging field conditions.

How We Delivered
1
The Challenge
Tight deadlines, cross-border field teams, unreliable network connectivity in remote areas, and a mid-project expansion of required data indicators by the client.
2
Tools Designed
Built customised KoboCollect instruments tailored to RAP indicators. Implemented offline capture protocols for areas without connectivity. Deployed supplementary tool mid-project for missing indicators.
3
Field Management
Monitored all incoming submissions daily. Directed field teams back to specific households for corrections. Applied analytical inference for derivable indicators — all documented and verified.
4
The Outcome
Complete, clean, verified dataset for all 762 PAPs delivered on schedule — forming the legal foundation for compensation decisions affecting hundreds of families across two countries.
