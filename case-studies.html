Case study 1 · Resettlement data management

Resettlement action plan data for a cross-border toll road.

An international environmental and social consultancy engaged All Your Needs to manage the full data lifecycle for a toll road resettlement action plan spanning two countries.

Context

The engagement

A resettlement action plan (RAP) sets out how people affected by a project will be compensated and supported. It depends on accurate records of every affected person and asset.

Project-affected person (PAP): anyone whose land, home, livelihood or assets are affected by a development project and who may be entitled to compensation.

Challenge

Tight deadlines and remote field teams

Field teams worked in remote areas across two countries on a compressed timeline. Network failures created gaps in the data flow. Midway through, the client asked for indicators that had not been part of the original questionnaire.

Approach

A new tool and careful inference

We built a separate data collection tool to go back and gather the missing information directly. Where an indicator could be derived from data already collected, we inferred and verified it rather than sending teams out again.

Result

Complete data, on schedule

All 762 project-affected persons were profiled across both countries, the additional indicators were delivered, and the data was handed over on schedule for the RAP.

Case study 3 · Data processing and GIS

1,663 savings groups, cleaned, coded and placed on the map.

Context

What a VSLA is

A village savings and loan association is a community group whose members save together and lend to one another. Knowing where groups are and how large they are helps programmes plan support.

Approach

Clean, code, then map

We merged two source datasets, resolved duplicate groups by county, district and name, and gave every group a unique county-based code. Each GPS point was then matched against official district boundaries so every group sits in the right district.

Records without coordinates were matched to official district names within their county, and anything that could not be confirmed was flagged for follow-up rather than guessed.

Result

Analysis-ready in one workbook

The client received a single workbook with the cleaned data, county and district summaries, the top districts, member-size distribution, a full log of every change and the original data preserved, plus presentation-quality charts in their brand colours.
