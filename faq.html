🛃 Customs Brokerage
A Customs Brokers Licence is a government-issued authorisation to clear goods through Liberian ports and airport on behalf of clients. Our licence covers all Liberian seaports — Freeport of Monrovia, Port of Buchanan, Port of Greenville, and Port of Harper — as well as Roberts International Airport. We handle both import and export clearance.
Customs clearance in Liberia requires professional knowledge of tariff classifications, LRA regulations, and documentation requirements. Using an unlicensed agent or attempting to self-clear can result in delays, fines, and seized goods. A licensed broker protects your cargo, ensures full legal compliance, and typically speeds up clearance significantly.
Yes. We offer priority clearance for time-sensitive shipments. Contact us directly at rerii@aynlr.com or call +231 777 969 916 to discuss urgent cargo requirements.
Yes. Many of our customs clients are international NGOs and development organisations bringing in programme equipment, vehicles, and supplies. We understand the specific documentation and exemption requirements that apply to these organisations and can advise on duty relief where applicable.
🥇 Gold Brokerage
Our Gold Brokers Licence, issued by the Government of Liberia, authorises All Your Needs to purchase gold from artisanal and small-scale miners for resale and export. All transactions are documented and fully compliant with the Minerals and Mining Act of Liberia.
Yes. International buyers who need a traceable, legally compliant Liberian gold supply chain can engage All Your Needs as their licensed broker. We manage the purchase, documentation, valuation, and facilitation of export — ensuring the buyer receives gold with a fully documented and compliant chain of custody.
Every transaction is fully documented with purchase receipts, weight and assay records, seller information, and compliance certificates as required by the Ministry of Mines and Energy. We prioritise transparency and can provide full transaction documentation to buyers on request.
