Privacy Policy

All Your Needs ("we", "our", "the Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at www.aynlr.com or contact us through any of our communication channels.

By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website contact form, email, or phone, we may collect:

  • Your name and job title
  • Your organisation or company name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Project details and descriptions you choose to share
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide in your communications with us

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may collect certain information automatically, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and the time and date of your visit. This information is used solely for improving website performance and user experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide information about our services
  • To prepare proposals, quotations, and service agreements
  • To manage our client relationships and deliver contracted services
  • To send you information relevant to your engagement with us
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • To improve our website and services

We will never use your personal information for unsolicited marketing or share it with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Information Sharing & Disclosure

All Your Needs does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service delivery: With trusted sub-contractors or partners who assist in delivering services you have engaged us for, subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Legal compliance: Where required by Liberian law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger or acquisition, subject to the same privacy protections
  • With your consent: In any other circumstance where you have explicitly authorised disclosure

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our server.

For client project data — particularly sensitive data collected during survey operations, RAP assessments, or other engagements — we apply industry-standard security practices including access controls, encrypted storage, and data handling protocols consistent with the requirements of our clients and funders (World Bank, AfDB, UNICEF, etc.).

While we implement these safeguards, no transmission of data over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to maintaining best practices.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@aynlr.com.
