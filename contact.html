Questions

Frequently asked.

Where do you operate?

Our survey and data operations cover all 15 counties of Liberia. Our leadership has also delivered engagements in the DRC, Zambia and Angola, and we are available for sub-contracts across West and Central Africa.

Who are your typical clients?

UN agencies, international development finance institutions, Liberian government ministries and agencies, international NGOs, environmental and social consultancies, infrastructure developers and private companies working in Liberia.

Is All Your Needs a registered Liberian company?

Yes. All Your Needs Incorporated is incorporated in the Republic of Liberia and holds government-issued licences for customs brokerage and gold brokerage.

Can you manage data collection outside Liberia?

Yes. Our leadership has managed data collection and resettlement data engagements in the DRC, Zambia and Angola.

Can you clear goods at any Liberian port?

Yes. Our customs brokers licence covers all Liberian seaports and Roberts International Airport, for import and export.
