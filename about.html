Who We Are

Liberia's Most Versatile Professional Services Firm

All Your Needs is a proudly Liberian professional services firm incorporated in 2021 under the laws of the Republic of Liberia. We were built around a simple but powerful conviction — that businesses, government institutions, and development organisations operating in Liberia deserve one trusted, capable partner across a comprehensive range of professional needs.

Headquartered in Monrovia, our leadership team brings personal professional experience spanning virtually every major national survey and census conducted in Liberia since the late 1990s. Our principals have worked alongside UNICEF, UNFPA, the World Bank, the African Development Bank, the European Union, the Ministry of Health, the National Elections Commission, and LISGIS — and that expertise now powers every All Your Needs engagement.

We are uniquely positioned among Liberian firms in holding both a Customs Brokers Licence and a Gold Brokers Licence — giving our clients immediate, legally compliant access to Liberia's trade and commodities markets.

Regulated & Compliant

Our Licensed Capabilities

All Your Needs holds two government-issued licences that are rare among Liberian firms — giving our clients direct, legally compliant access to services that most competitors simply cannot offer.

🛃
Customs Brokers Licence

We are licensed by the Government of Liberia to clear goods on behalf of clients through all Liberian seaports — Freeport of Monrovia, Buchanan Port, Greenville Port, Harper Port — and through Roberts International Airport, for both import and export. We manage all documentation, tariff classification, duty calculation, and liaison with the Liberia Revenue Authority.

🥇
Gold Brokers Licence

We hold a government-issued licence to purchase gold from artisanal and small-scale miners for resale and export. All transactions are fully compliant with Liberian minerals regulations and documented transparently. We liaise directly with the Ministry of Mines and Energy and can support international buyers seeking a compliant, traceable Liberian supply chain.
